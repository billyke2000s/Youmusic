YouTube stops the music the moment you leave the app, unless you pay for Premium.
YouMusic is a free, tiny Android app that fixes that. It gives you the whole of
YouTube, and it plays in the background like a real music app.

## What you get

**🔒 Background play.** Screen off, other apps open, phone in your pocket. It keeps going.

**🎛️ Real music controls.** Song name, channel and cover art in your notifications
and on your lock screen, with skip, pause and a seek bar, just like Spotify.

**🎧 Headphones just work.** Bluetooth and headphone buttons control playback, and
pulling your headphones out pauses it.

**📋 Paste and play.** Open the app to one giant box. Paste any YouTube link and you're listening.

**🌐 All of YouTube.** Prefer browsing? One tap opens the normal YouTube home page,
with search, your feed and everything else.

**📤 Share straight in.** Find something in the YouTube app, tap **Share → YouMusic**,
and it starts playing.

**🪶 Featherweight.** Under 50 KB. No account, no tracking, no ads of its own.

**[Download YouMusic.apk](https://github.com/billyke2000s/Youmusic/releases/latest/download/YouMusic.apk)**

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>The music stops when my screen locks</b></summary>

Some phones close background apps to save battery. Open **Settings → Apps →
YouMusic → Battery** and choose **Unrestricted**. Keep YouMusic's notifications
turned on too, because they're what keeps it running.

Still stopping? [Let us know](https://github.com/billyke2000s/Youmusic/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)
which phone you have.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I stop it completely?</b></summary>

Tap **✕** on the notification, or swipe YouMusic away from your recent apps.
Pressing back or home just tucks it into the background so the music carries on.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What about ads?</b></summary>

YouTube's ads still play, but you can drag the seek bar in the notification to the
end of an ad to get past it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I sign in to my YouTube account?</b></summary>

Usually, yes. Tap **Browse YouTube** and sign in as normal. Google occasionally
refuses sign-in inside apps like this one, but YouMusic works fine without an
account.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does my phone warn me when I install it?</b></summary>

Android warns about every app that doesn't come from the Play Store. YouMusic's
code is all public right here, so anyone can see exactly what it does. It only
talks to YouTube.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I save songs to listen offline?</b></summary>

Not yet. YouMusic streams from YouTube.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is there an iPhone version?</b></summary>

No, Android only.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What permissions does it use?</b></summary>

Internet (to load YouTube), notifications (for the music controls), and permission
to keep running in the background. Nothing else: no location, contacts, camera,
microphone or files.
</details>
