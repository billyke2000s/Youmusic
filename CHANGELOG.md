# What's new in YouMusic

## 1.1

**Music now keeps playing when you leave the app.** Press home, switch apps or
lock your screen, and it carries on.

- A proper music player in your notifications and on your lock screen, with the
  song name, cover art, a seek bar and ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ ✕ buttons
- Headphone and Bluetooth buttons now control playback
- Pulling out your headphones pauses the music
- If YouTube pops up "Continue watching?" while you're away, YouMusic answers it
  for you
- Pressing back no longer closes the player; it tucks it into the background, like
  Spotify. Tap ✕ on the notification to stop.

## 1.0.5

- Future updates now install straight over the old version.
  **Coming from 1.0.4 or earlier? Uninstall that once first.**

## 1.0

The first version.

- Paste any YouTube link into the big box and play it
- Browse the full YouTube home page inside the app
- Share videos from the YouTube app straight to YouMusic
- Works with normal links, short links, YouTube Music, Shorts, live streams and
  playlists
- Fullscreen video
